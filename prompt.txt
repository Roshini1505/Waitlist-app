11. INTELLIGENT QUEUE AND RANKING ENGINE

Build a configurable ranking engine.

Do not use the old simplistic position-decrementing algorithm.

A user must never receive an independently decremented queue position that causes duplicate or inconsistent positions.

Use score-based ranking.

Potential scoring signals:

- Approved referrals.
- Verified email.
- Verified phone.
- Campaign engagement.
- Configured campaign bonuses.
- Approved promotional activities.

Build configurable scoring rules.

Ranking requirements

- Score calculation.
- Deterministic tie-breaking.
- Real-time leaderboard.
- Ranking history.
- Position lookup.
- Ranking recalculation.
- Redis Sorted Set integration.
- Cache rebuild capability.
- Concurrent update protection.
- Campaign-level ranking isolation.

PostgreSQL remains authoritative.

Redis is an acceleration layer.

Do not allow Redis state to become the only record of a customer's position or score.

Define how score updates, Redis synchronization and recovery work.

Ensure that concurrent referral approvals cannot lose score updates.

Provide tests for ranking consistency.

13. FRAUD DETECTION ENGINE

Build a rule-based fraud risk scoring system.

Do not claim to have AI fraud detection unless a real machine-learning or AI component is implemented and evaluated.

Signals may include:

- Disposable email domains.
- Repeated registration velocity.
- Suspicious device reuse.
- Unusual referral patterns.
- Multiple suspicious accounts.
- Repeated failed verification.
- Abnormal campaign activity.

Implement configurable risk scores.

Suggested actions:

- Normal: Automatically approve subject to validation.
- Suspicious: Hold referral credit.
- High risk: Require additional verification.
- Critical: Flag for administrator investigation.

Avoid automatic account bans based on one weak signal.

- Risk scoring.
- Risk explanation.
